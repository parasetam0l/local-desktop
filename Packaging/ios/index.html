LocalDesktop

Control your Mac from your iPhone or iPad on your local network.

Install on this device

Version {{VERSION}} · iOS 17 or later

Open this page in Safari on the iPhone or iPad you want to install it on.

  1. Tap Install on this device, then Install.
  2. The app appears on the Home Screen. Installing over an older version keeps your paired Macs and settings.
  3. On the Mac, use LocalDesktop {{VERSION}} as well: release page.

This build installs only on devices registered with the developer account; on any other device iOS reports that it can't install the app.
